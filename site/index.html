How it works

You keep talking to your agent as you do now. Baba gives the work a place of its own: a page where you see what the agent sees, as it changes.

You can act on the page too. The agent is told what you did, and builds on it.

Getting started

You need a coding agent, such as Claude Code. Copy this to it, in the project you want Baba in:

Install babavoss in this project: read https://babavoss.org/llms.txt and follow it.

Then say what you want

In your own words. “I want a surface…”

Software development

  • …that tracks what still blocks the next release.
  • …that lists every failing test with the change that broke it.
  • …where I approve your database changes one by one.

Running a business

  • …that matches each month's payments to our invoices.
  • …that shows which subscriptions we pay for and when each renews.
  • …that keeps every open customer request with who answers it.

Research and writing

  • …that collects the papers we read, with your summary and my verdict.
  • …that compares five interviews answer by answer.
  • …that tracks each chapter's draft, its open questions and its sources.

Your agent builds it and opens it on your page.
